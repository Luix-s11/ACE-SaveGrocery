Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How do I place an order?

Browse our grocery products, add your desired items to your cart, proceed to checkout, and select your payment method.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?

We suuport available payment options such as Visa and MasterCard.

Q: How can I track my order?

Visit the Track My Order section and enter your order number to check its status.

Q: Can I cancel my order?

Orders may be canceled before they are processed or dispatched, depending on their status.

Q: What if an item is unavailable?

Unavailable products may be removed from your order or handled according to our substitution policy.

Q: How can I contact customer support?

Visit the Contact Customer Support section for assistance with your concerns.



Track My Order

Keep track of your grocery purchases and delivery status.

Order Placed: Your order has been successfully submitted.
Processing: We are preparing your groceries.
Ready for Dispatch: Your order is packed and ready for delivery.
Out for Delivery: Your order is on its way to your address.
Delivered: Your order has arrived.


To check your order status, use your order number or contact our customer support team. Delivery estimates may vary depending on your location and order volume.



Delivery & Shipping

We aim to deliver your groceries conveniently and on time.

Delivery Areas: Delivery is available in selected service areas.
Delivery Fees: Fees may vary depending on your location and order total.
Delivery Schedule: Available delivery dates and time slots are shown during checkout.
Order Preparation: Fresh and packaged items are prepared for delivery after your order is confirmed.
Delays: Weather conditions, traffic, and high order volumes may affect delivery times.


Please ensure that your delivery address and contact number are correct before placing your order.



Payments & Refunds

We provide convenient payment options to make grocery shopping easier.

Accepted Payment Methods:

Visa
MasterCard

Payment options depend on availability at checkout.

Refund Policy

Refund requests may be considered for duplicate charges, canceled eligible orders, missing items, or products received damaged or incorrectly.

Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider. Please provide your order number and a description of the issue when submitting a request.

Never share your card CVV, PIN, password, or one-time password with customer support.



Orders & Cancellations

Customers can review their grocery orders before completing checkout.

Placing an Order

  1. Browse the available products.
  2. Add items to your shopping cart.
  3. Review quantities and prices.
  4. Enter your delivery information.
  5. Select a payment method.
  6. Confirm your order.

Order Cancellation

Cancellation may be available while an order is still being processed. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If you need to change your order, contact customer support as soon as possible. Refund eligibility depends on the order status and applicable policies.



Returns & Damaged Items

We want you to receive the groceries you ordered in good condition.

Please contact customer support if you receive:

  • Damaged or spoiled products
  • Incorrect items
  • Missing items
  • Products that do not match your order

How to Report an Issue

  1. Provide your order number.
  2. Describe the problem with the item.
  3. Include photos when appropriate.
  4. Submit your concern to customer support.

Our team will review your request and explain the available resolution, which may include a replacement, refund, or other appropriate action under our policy. Perishable items may have special reporting requirements.
